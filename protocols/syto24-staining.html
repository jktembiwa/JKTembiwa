SYTO 24 DNA staining of M. smegmatis

A quick live-cell nucleoid stain with no wash step.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

Reagent: SYTO 24 green fluorescent nucleic acid stain (Thermo Fisher, catalog S7559). The stock is a liquid. Dilute 1:10 in DMSO and aliquot (2 µL and 5 µL) into PCR tubes. Store in a foil-covered box at –20 °C in a desiccator.

  1. Stain with other dyes first (e.g. HADA) and complete their washes.
  2. Add 1 µL diluted SYTO 24 to 100 µL culture.
  3. Mount and image. No wash is needed.

The dye is very bright. Reduce exposure settings so images are not saturated (see Image management).

Source / credit: Boutte Lab protocol (as received), adapted by K. Tembiwa.
