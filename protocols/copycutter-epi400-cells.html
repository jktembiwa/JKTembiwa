CopyCutter EPI400 cells for unstable or toxic plasmids

Using low-copy-number competent cells (and the induction solution) to clone toxic or unstable sequences, then raising copy number for plasmid preps.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

What they are

E. coli with the plasmid-copy-number gene pcnB under an inducible promoter. ColE1/pMB1-origin plasmids (pUC, pET types) are kept at low copy number (about 9 copies for pUC19, versus ~216 in the parent strain) until the CopyCutter Induction Solution is added, which raises copy number (pUC19 ~200 copies). Genotype: mcrA Δ(mrr-hsdRMS-mcrBC) recA1 endA1 ΔpcnB, T1-phage resistant, blue/white compatible.

Cautions

  • Plasmids are distributed randomly to daughter cells at low copy; 10–60% of cells can lack plasmid. Kanamycin selection gives fewer plasmid-free cells than ampicillin.
  • Not recommended for vectors that need tetracycline selection.
  • Store cells at –70 °C; do not refreeze chemically competent cells.

Electroporation

  1. DNA in water or low-salt buffer (e.g. TE). Prepare 1 mL antibiotic-free LB per sample; pre-chill cuvettes.
  2. Thaw 50 µL cells on ice; mix gently with DNA (avoid bubbles); electroporate with your E. coli settings.
  3. Immediately add 950 µL room-temperature LB, move to a 15 mL tube, shake at 37 °C, 220–230 rpm for 1 h.
  4. Plate on LB with antibiotic. Control: 10 pg pUC19 (dilute the supplied 100 pg/µL DNA 1:10 and use 1 µL); plate on ampicillin 100 µg/mL.

Chemical transformation

Standard (highest efficiency): thaw on ice; add 1–5 µL DNA/ligation; ice 5–30 min; 42 °C for 30 s; ice 2 min; add 250 µL SOC; 37 °C for 60 min with shaking (225 rpm); plate. Five-minute procedure (ampicillin only; ≥10-fold lower efficiency): add 3–5 µL DNA, ice 5 min, spread the whole mix on a pre-warmed LB + ampicillin (100 µg/mL) plate.

Inducing to high copy number

  1. Grow a 5 mL overnight (LB + antibiotic) from a colony.
  2. Dilute the overnight 1:10 to read OD, then dilute to OD₆₀₀ 0.2 in LB + antibiotic + 1× CopyCutter Induction Solution (supplied at 1000×).
  3. Shake vigorously at 37 °C for 4 h. Aeration matters: use at least 15 mL tubes for 5 mL cultures and 125 mL flasks for 50 mL.
  4. Isolate plasmid DNA by your usual method.

Screen clones by PCR while uninduced (few copies per cell). See also the FAQ on toxic genes.
