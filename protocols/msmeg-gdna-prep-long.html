High-quality genomic DNA from M. smegmatis (delipidation, lysozyme, proteinase K)

A long, higher-yield genomic DNA prep with chloroform:methanol delipidation and overnight lysozyme treatment.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

Handle phenol, chloroform and methanol in a fume hood and dispose as hazardous waste. For a fast, lower-yield alternative see Smash & Grab.

Reagents: TE (10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8, 1 mM EDTA); 1× TBS; phenol:chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (25:24:1); chloroform:methanol (2:1); 1 M Tris base pH 9; lysozyme 10 mg/mL; 10% SDS; proteinase K; chloroform; chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (24:1); DNase-free RNase A; 3 M sodium acetate; 100% and 70% ethanol (ice-cold); 55 °C water bath.

  1. Harvest 5–10 mL saturated culture, up to 60 mL log-phase culture. Optional: freeze the pellet at –80 °C for at least 4 h (weakens the cell wall; not required).
  2. Resuspend in 500 µL TE (EDTA protects DNA from nucleases).
  3. Add an equal volume of chloroform:methanol (2:1) and rock at room temperature at least 1 h. This removes cell wall lipids.
  4. Spin 2,500 × g, 20 min. The cells form a tight band at the interface. Remove the top layer; push a pipette tip through the cell layer at the side to remove the bottom layer, leaving the packed band (decanting tends to lose cells).
  5. Leave the uncapped tube at 55 °C for 10–15 min to evaporate solvent traces that inhibit lysozyme. Do not over-dry.
  6. Add 500 µL TE and vortex vigorously (the cells clump after organic extraction).
  7. Add 0.1 volume 1 M Tris base pH 9.
  8. Add 0.1 volume lysozyme stock and incubate 12–16 h at 37 °C. Do not vortex after adding lysozyme (shears DNA).
  9. Next day, place 100% ethanol at –20 °C.
  10. Loosen any chunks from the bottom of the tube with a pipette tip.
  11. Add 1 µL RNase (10 mg/mL) for ~20 µg/mL, 37 °C at least 30 min. Some RNA still co-precipitates and affects A260/280 readings.
  12. Add 0.1 volume 10% SDS and 0.01 volume proteinase K stock (the original text gives ~200 µg/mL final; check against your stock) and incubate 3 h at 55 °C, mixing by inversion.
  13. Add an equal volume of phenol:chloroform:isoamyl alcohol. Pipette the whole mixture, not only the isoamyl alcohol layer that floats on top. Mix, spin.
  14. Move the top aqueous layer to a new tube, avoiding the white interface.
  15. Repeat 13–14 until no protein is visible at the interface (usually one more time). Add ~150 µL TE before each extraction to keep the aqueous phase at 500–700 µL.
  16. Remove residual phenol with an equal volume of chloroform; spin; keep the aqueous layer.
  17. Add 0.1 volume 3 M sodium acetate and mix. Add 2–3 volumes ice-cold 100% ethanol, mix, keep at –20 °C for 10 min to overnight.
  18. Spin at maximum speed for 10 min at 4 °C. Wash the pellet 2–3 times with ice-cold 70% ethanol.
  19. Air-dry briefly and dissolve in 50–100 µL TE.

Sodium acetate is pH 5.2. Original step 1 also has details not carried over here.
