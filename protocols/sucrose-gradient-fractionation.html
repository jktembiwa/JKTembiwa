Sucrose gradient fractionation of mycobacterial membranes (nitrogen cavitation)

Lyse M. smegmatis by nitrogen cavitation, separate membrane domains on a 20–50% sucrose gradient, collect fractions and analyze by western blot.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

Cell lysate

  1. Inoculate 5 mL of seed culture into each of three 2 L flasks with 500 mL 7H9 (glycerol, supplement, 0.05% Tween 80) and grow at 30 °C for ~16–18 h.
  2. At mid-log (OD₆₀₀ 0.5–1.0), centrifuge at 8,000 rpm for 15 min at 4 °C in large bottles.
  3. Resuspend each pellet in 50 mM HEPES/NaOH pH 7.4 and move to a pre-weighed 50 mL conical.
  4. Spin 4,000 rpm, 10 min, 4 °C. Resuspend in 40 mL HEPES buffer; spin again; weigh the pellet.
  5. Resuspend at 1 g per 5 mL lysis buffer (25 mM HEPES pH 7.4, 20% sucrose, 2 mM EGTA; recipe below) with 1/25 volume protease inhibitor cocktail (EDTA-free).
  6. Nitrogen cavitation: 1,850 psi (up to 2,200 psi) for 30 min with gentle swirling every 10 min. Release the pressure and collect. Repeat three times.
  7. Spin 4,000 rpm for 10 min at 4 °C. Move the supernatant to a new tube (avoid the top “marshmallow” layer) and spin again.

Gradient

  1. While cavitating, pour a 20–50% (w/v) linear sucrose gradient (gradient maker; long program), with 25 mM HEPES pH 7.4 in each solution.
  2. Layer 1.2 mL cleared lysate on top (200 µL × 6). Keep the rest as unfractionated (UF) material.
  3. Centrifuge in an SW40 swing-bucket rotor at 35,000 rpm (~218,000 × g) for 6 h at 4 °C.
  4. Collect fractions with a gradient fractionator (about 14 fractions, 0.5 speed, 6.67 mm per fraction) on an ice-water chamber. Wash the system with 50% ethanol then water afterwards.
  5. Aliquot 450 µL of each fraction into two tubes, snap-freeze in liquid nitrogen, and store at –80 °C. Keep ~100 µL for analysis.

Analysis

  1. Read the refractive index of 20 µL of each fraction to confirm the sucrose gradient.
  2. Mix 12 µL of each fraction with 4 µL 4× loading buffer, heat 95 °C for 5 min, and run all fractions on one gel (25 mA per gel) with a pre-stained standard.
  3. Transfer to PVDF in methanol-containing transfer buffer (110 V, 1 h, or 14 V overnight). Stain with Ponceau S, image, destain.
  4. Block 1 h in 5% milk in PBS + 0.05% Tween 20. Primary (example: anti-HA, 1:2,000), 1 h. Wash 3 × 10 min. HRP secondary (1:2,000), 1 h. Wash 3 × 10 min. Develop with ECL.

Solutions

  • 1 M HEPES pH 7.4: 23.8 g in ~80 mL water, adjust pH, make to 100 mL; store at –20 °C.
  • 50% sucrose in 25 mM HEPES: 50 g sucrose + 2.5 mL 1 M HEPES + water to 100 mL; store at –20 °C.
  • 20% sucrose in 25 mM HEPES: 20 g sucrose + 2.5 mL 1 M HEPES + water to 100 mL; store at –20 °C.
  • 100 mM EGTA: 3.80 g in ~80 mL water; adjust to pH 8.0 to dissolve; make to 100 mL; store at 4 °C.
  • Lysis buffer (45 mL): 675 µL 1 M HEPES, 18 mL 50% sucrose, 900 µL 100 mM EGTA, 25.43 mL water.
