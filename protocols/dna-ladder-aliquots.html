Preparing DNA ladder aliquots

Dilute a stock DNA ladder with TE and loading dye so it is ready to load.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

Mix:

  • 1 mL DNA ladder
  • 1 mL TE buffer pH 7.4
  • 1 mL 6× loading dye
  • 3 mL nuclease-free water

Mix gently by pipetting. Make six 1 mL aliquots and store at –20 °C. Load 6 µL per lane.

Check the dilution against the ladder’s concentration; the same recipe may not suit every ladder.
