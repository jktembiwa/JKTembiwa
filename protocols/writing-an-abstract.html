Six-sentence structure for an abstract

A six-sentence template for a scientific abstract.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

  1. Background and why it matters.
  2. Gap: the question you are addressing.
  3. Sentences 3–5: key conclusions, with a mention of the approach. Do not list individual experimental results; say what you learned from them.
  4. Sentence 6: why the results are important.
