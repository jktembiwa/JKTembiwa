Optimizing recombinant kinase expression and lysis (example: PknA)

A two-temperature test induction with normalized pre/post-induction, supernatant and pellet samples; a fillable worksheet for a kinase such as PknA.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

For the general method see Test inductions for protein expression. This page is the worksheet-style version for two expression temperatures.

Day 1

  1. Grow a 5 mL starter of the expression strain with the plasmid antibiotics (example: chloramphenicol 25 µg/mL + ampicillin 140 µg/mL). Record the OD.
  2. Add 2.5 mL starter to each of two flasks (A, B) of 50 mL LB + ampicillin. Grow at 37 °C to OD₆₀₀ 0.6. Always grow at 37 °C before adding IPTG, then move to the induction temperature.
  3. Record pre-induction OD for A and B. Save a pre-induction sample: 500 µL culture + 100 µL 6× Laemmli (Pre-A, Pre-B).
  4. Add 1 mM IPTG to both. Express flask A at 25 °C and flask B at 18 °C for ~16 h.

Day 2

  1. Read the OD of A and B. To load equal cell equivalents, compute X = OD(post) ÷ OD(pre), then take 500/X µL of culture and make up to 500 µL with water (500 − 500/X µL). Example: post 6, pre 0.6 → X = 10 → 50 µL culture + 450 µL water.
  2. Store as Post-A / Post-B in 100 µL 6× Laemmli. Heat 95 °C, 10 min.
  3. Pellet the cultures and freeze at –20 °C.
  4. Gel: 20 µL each of pre-A, post-A, pre-B, post-B.

Lysis of the pellets

  1. Resuspend each pellet in 30 mL buffer (50 mM Tris pH 7.5, 150 mM NaCl, 20% glycerol). Add 300 µL 100 mM PMSF and a pinch of lysozyme; 30 min on ice.
  2. Sonicate (power 4, 10 pulses of 30 s, 10 s rests).
  3. Centrifuge 40 min at 12,000 rpm. Move supernatants to new tubes (sup-25 °C, sup-18 °C).
  4. Mix 500 µL supernatant with 100 µL 6× Laemmli (Sup-A, Sup-B).
  5. Resuspend each pellet in 30 mL buffer; mix 500 µL with 100 µL 6× Laemmli (Pellet-A, Pellet-B).
  6. Heat all samples 90–95 °C for 10 min. Run on a gel to compare soluble and insoluble fractions at each temperature.
