Setting up a 96-well growth curve (dilution calculator)

Calculate culture and medium volumes to start every well at the same OD, with a fillable template.

Version 1.0

Which one should I use?

Other pages cover related versions or steps:

  • Manual growth curves (flask or tube, OD600 over time): Growth curve by hand in flasks or tubes, measuring OD600 over time.
  • Calculating doubling times from growth curves in GraphPad Prism: Analyze either type of growth curve for doubling time in GraphPad Prism.

Purpose

Start every well of a plate-reader growth curve at the same initial OD₆₀₀ by diluting each culture into medium. The calculator below gives the volumes of culture and 7H9 for each strain. The default is 200 µL final volume and an initial OD of 0.1.

Calculator

# Strain / genotype Culture OD₆₀₀ Culture (µL) 7H9 (µL)

Culture volume = (initial OD × final volume) ÷ culture OD. Medium volume = final volume − culture volume. If a culture’s OD is below the target, it cannot be diluted to the target and the row shows “OD too low”.

Plate reader settings (temperature, shaking, reading interval, duration) and plate layout notes should be specified for each experiment.
