Standard restriction–ligation cloning

Digest, purify, ligate and transform for traditional cloning; Gibson assembly is usually preferred.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

Not the preferred approach: Gibson assembly is easier and usually better.

  1. Digest 1 µg of vector and the PCR product.
  2. Gel-purify the vector backbone (gel purification). The PCR product can be column-purified. Elute in 30 µL water for higher yields.
  3. Ligate with fresh T4 ligase buffer at a 3:1 insert:vector molar ratio. Ng insert = 3 × (kb insert) ÷ (kb vector) × 50 ng vector.
  4. Ligate in 10 µL with 0.5 µL T4 ligase, overnight at 16 °C or 3 hours at room temperature.
  5. For electroporation, dialyze the whole ligation on a membrane over water for 30 min (not needed for chemically competent cells).
  6. Thaw E. coli competent cells on ice for 15 min. Electroporate the entire dialyzed ligation in a cold cuvette, or add the ligation to chemically competent cells and heat shock for 1 min.
  7. Recover electroporated cells in 1 mL LB at 37 °C for 1 hour.
  8. Spin at maximum speed for 30 s, pour off the LB, resuspend in the remaining liquid and streak onto a prewarmed plate.
