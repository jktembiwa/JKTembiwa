Making electrocompetent E. coli

Overnight starter to frozen electrocompetent aliquots, with glycerol washes.

Version 1.0

  1. Grow a starter culture overnight (add the appropriate antibiotic if you want to select against contaminants).
  2. In the morning, dilute to OD₆₀₀ of at least 0.05 in LB without antibiotics. Grow to OD₆₀₀ ~0.8 (up to 1.0 is fine).
  3. Pellet at 5,000 × g for 10 min at 4 °C. Pour off the supernatant.
  4. Wash 1: ice-cold sterile 10% glycerol, half the original culture volume. Resuspend well and pellet again as above.
  5. Wash 2: ice-cold 10% glycerol, a quarter of the original volume. Pellet again.
  6. Resuspend in enough ice-cold 10% glycerol to give 1/100 of the original culture volume (e.g. for 200 mL, use 1.5–1.75 mL to reach about 2 mL).
  7. Use fresh, or aliquot 50 µL, snap-freeze in a dry ice / 95% ethanol bath for 30 s and store at –80 °C.

Keep cells and tubes cold throughout. See Electrocompetent cells and electroporation for settings.
