Mapping a single transposon insertion (SacII digest, ligation, pir+ E. coli)

Recover the transposon–genome junction from one mutant by digesting gDNA, self-ligating, transforming into a pir+ E. coli strain and sequencing out of the transposon.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

For an individual mutant from a φMycoMarT7 library (see the Tn-seq overview). The idea is that a SacII-cut, re-ligated fragment containing the transposon can replicate as a plasmid in a pir+ host and carry the flanking genomic sequence with it. Presumably the transposon carries an R6K origin and kanamycin marker; confirm against the phMycoMarT7 map.

  1. Prepare genomic DNA with a quick prep that includes RNase (so the A₂₆₀ reading reflects DNA; see Smash and Grab or the long gDNA prep).
  2. Digest 2 µg gDNA with 3 µL SacII in 1× CutSmart buffer (3 µL), water to 30 µL, overnight at 37 °C.
  3. Heat-inactivate 20 min at 65 °C.
  4. Add 4 µL T4 DNA ligase buffer and 2 µL T4 DNA ligase; incubate overnight at 16 °C.
  5. Heat-inactivate 20 min at 65 °C.
  6. Reduce the volume to ~15 µL (speed-vac) and dialyze the DNA for 30 min.
  7. Electroporate into freshly made electrocompetent pir+ E. coli (BW25142; see E. coli electrocompetent cells).
  8. Pick colonies, grow in LB + kanamycin 50 µg/mL, miniprep.
  9. Sequence with the transposon primer that reads out of the transposon into the genome: 5′-GTCTAGAGACCGGGGACTTATC-3′ (the same transposon primer used for Tn-seq library PCR 1).

Align the resulting sequence to the genome to find the insertion site.

Source / credit: Protocol from the Rego lab, itself adapted from an earlier lab protocol (Mike Chao); received through the Boutte lab. Condensed by K. Tembiwa.
