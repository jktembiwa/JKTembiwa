ZipTip C18 clean-up of peptide samples for mass spectrometry

Desalt and concentrate a peptide digest on a µ-C18 ZipTip and elute in 4 µL for MALDI-TOF or ESI-Q-TOF.

Use µ-C18 ZipTips to clean up peptide samples. Add link to the manufacturer’s ZipTip user guide.

Before starting - The sample must be aqueous. If dry, rehydrate in 1% formic acid / 2% acetonitrile. - Use only polypropylene microcentrifuge tubes certified low in leachables (the handout specifies one brand of 1.5 mL polypropylene tubes); other tubes can leach plasticizers that interfere with mass spec. - Make solutions fresh daily. Use a new ZipTip for every sample. - Aliquot 4 µL of elution buffer (60% acetonitrile / 0.1% formic acid) for each sample first, to avoid contamination. - Avoid drawing air through the tip from equilibration to elution; if bubbles form, pull buffers more slowly.

Procedure (pipettor set to 10 µL, tip attached) 1. Equilibrate: aspirate and discard 3 × 100% acetonitrile, then 3 × 0.1% formic acid. 2. Load: pipette the digest up and down 10 × (returning it to its tube). 3. Wash: aspirate and discard 6 × 0.1% formic acid. 4. Elute: pipette up and down 10 × in the 4 µL elution buffer (return to the same tube). The sample is now desalted and concentrated for MALDI-TOF or ESI-Q-TOF.

If not running mass spec the same day, freeze at −80 °C.

Source / credit: Lab handout (author not recorded); uses the Millipore ZipTip product. See the manufacturer's ZipTip user guide for the full protocol.
