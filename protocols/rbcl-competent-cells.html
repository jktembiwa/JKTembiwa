Rubidium chloride chemically competent E. coli

Prepare rubidium chloride chemically competent E. coli, snap-freeze for storage, and transform by heat shock.

Version 1.0

Buffers and media

LB (1 L): Autoclave; add antibiotic after the medium cools.

Component Amount
Tryptone 10 g
NaCl 10 g
Yeast extract 5 g
Agar (for plates) 20 g

Φ broth (500 mL): Autoclave.

Component Amount Concentration
Tryptone 10 g 2%
KCl 0.37 g 10 mM
Yeast extract 2.5 g 0.5%
MgSO₄ 2.5 g 0.4%

TJB1 (250 mL): Adjust to pH 5.8 with HCl, slowly and without overshooting; adding NaOH to correct will precipitate salts. Filter sterilize and store at 4 °C.

Component Amount Concentration
RbCl 3.0 g 100 mM
MnCl₂·4H₂O 2.47 g 50 mM
Potassium acetate 0.74 g 30 mM
CaCl₂·2H₂O 0.36 g 10 mM
Glycerol 100% 37.5 mL 15%

TJB2 (100 mL): Adjust to pH 7.0 with NaOH. Filter sterilize and store at 4 °C.

Component Amount Concentration
RbCl 0.12 g 10 mM
MOPS 0.20 g 10 mM
CaCl₂·2H₂O 1.10 g 75 mM
Glycerol 100% 15 mL 15%

Preparing the cells

Day 1. Grow 5 mL of the strain overnight in LB.

Day 2.

  1. Inoculate 1 mL of the overnight culture into each of 2 flasks containing 100 mL Φ broth.
  2. Shake at 30 °C until OD₆₀₀ reaches 0.50 (2–3 hours).
  3. Chill the cultures on ice for 10 min.
  4. Transfer to four 50 mL conical tubes.
  5. Centrifuge at 4 °C, 3000 rpm (1900 × g), for 5 min and discard the supernatant.
  6. Resuspend each pellet in 15 mL ice-cold TJB1 (shaking the tubes works well). Chill on ice for 5 min.
  7. Centrifuge at 4 °C, 3000 rpm (1900 × g), for 5 min and discard the supernatant.
  8. Resuspend each pellet in 2 mL ice-cold TJB2.
  9. Snap-freeze 100 µL (or 50 µL) aliquots in liquid nitrogen. Do not use ethanol–dry ice.
  10. Store at −80 °C.

Transformation

  1. Thaw one tube of cells per transformation on ice and resuspend by gently tapping the tube.
  2. Add DNA and mix by swirling the pipette tip. Do not pipette up and down.
  3. Incubate on ice for 20 min.
  4. Heat shock in a 42 °C water bath for 45 s.
  5. Return to ice for 2 min.
  6. Add 200 µL SOC (or LB) and shake at 37 °C for 45 min.
  7. Plate the entire contents of the tube on LB agar with the appropriate antibiotic.

Source / credit: Rubidium chloride method (after Hanahan, 1983); adapted by K. Tembiwa from a lab protocol.
