In vitro kinase assay with radiolabeled ATP (Pkn kinases and substrates)

Phosphorylate purified substrates with PknA and/or PknB in the presence of radiolabeled ATP, resolve on SDS-PAGE, and detect by phosphorimaging.

Draft: not yet reviewed by the author Draft 0.1

Highlighted values are awaiting confirmation. Do not rely on them without checking.

Radioactivity: work only in an approved radioisotope area, with training, shielding, monitoring and waste procedures from your institution’s radiation safety office.

Set-up

Kinase buffer: 50 mM Tris pH 7.5, 250 mM NaCl, 10% glycerol. Use a kinase:substrate mass ratio of 1:10 (5 µg substrate to 0.5 µg kinase per 20 µL reaction).

  1. Thaw one aliquot of each protein on ice. Measure the concentration of each with the kinase buffer as blank.
  2. Label tubes. Example set for testing a substrate against two kinases:
    • substrate only (no-kinase negative control), one for each substrate
    • kinase + known substrate (positive controls, e.g. PknA–Wag31, PknB–CwlM)
    • PknA + test substrate
    • PknB + test substrate
    • PknA + PknB + test substrate
  3. Add MnCl₂ to 1 mM final (0.8 µL of a 25 mM stock into 20 µL).
  4. Add the volume of each protein that gives the desired mass (µg ÷ mg/mL = µL) and kinase buffer to 20 µL total. Worked example: 5 µg of substrate at 10 mg/mL = 0.5 µL; 0.5 µg of kinase at 4.2 mg/mL = 0.12 µL.
  5. In the radioactive area, add hot ATP at 0.182 µCi/µL final: prepare a mixture of 3 µL hot ATP + 5.5 µL kinase buffer and add 1 µL to each tube. Isotope, stock activity and µCi per reaction need to be filled in. Incubate 30 min at room temperature.
  6. Immediately add 5 µL 6× Laemmli. Do not heat.

Gel and imaging

  1. Load 10 µL on a 12% SDS-PAGE gel. Run about 50 min at 35 mA, then 45 mA. Run the dye front off the gel, so that unincorporated ATP runs out and does not blow out the signal.
  2. Stain for 1 h, destain 2–3 h, and photograph the gel.
  3. Sandwich the gel between two sheets of clear filter paper wetted with 40% ethanol (prevents cracking). Squeegee out bubbles, clamp between plastic sheets and dry in a hood overnight.
  4. Blank a phosphor screen by placing it white side against a light box for ~1 h.
  5. Cut the plastic to fit, place the dried gel against the white side of the screen in the cassette, oriented right-side up, and label the cassette (name, date).
  6. Expose overnight (about 8 h can suffice) and scan on a phosphorimager.

Controls

Include substrate-only, kinase-only (autophosphorylation) and known kinase–substrate pairs. Compare the autoradiograph to the stained gel to confirm that the signal is at the substrate band.
