"Smash & Grab": quick genomic DNA from M. smegmatis

A fast (30–45 min) bead-beating and phenol–chloroform prep that gives enough genomic DNA for 50–100 PCRs.

1.0

Fast, but yield is lower and DNA more sheared than in longer protocols. Enough for 50–100 PCRs and some downstream uses (e.g. transposon mapping of isolated clones). See also Nucleic acid extraction from M. smegmatis.

Handle phenol and chloroform in a fume hood and dispose of them as hazardous waste.

  1. Pellet 10 mL of log-phase (OD 0.3 to 0.8) overnight culture. Resuspend in 400 µL 10 mM Tris pH 9 and transfer to a bead-beating tube with glass beads.
  2. Add 800 µL phenol:chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (25:24:1, pH 8.0). Bead-beat 2 × 45 s.
  3. Spin 14,000 × g, 5 min. Transfer the upper aqueous layer (~150–300 µL; should be colorless) to a new tube.
  4. Add 1 µL 1 mg/mL RNase A; 37 °C for 30 min. (RNA co-precipitates and interferes with A260/280 quantification; skip if this does not matter.)
  5. Add 0.1 volume 3 M sodium acetate (pH 5.2), mix.
  6. Add 1 mL ice-cold ethanol, mix, and keep at –20 °C until you spin. Pellet 5–10 min at 14,000 × g in the cold.
  7. Remove ethanol. Wash the pellet with 1 mL ice-cold 70% ethanol, spin 5 min at room temperature, aspirate. Wash 4–5 more times with 70% ethanol (no spin).
  8. Spin 30 s to remove residual ethanol; dry 30 s to a few minutes. Resuspend in 50 µL water.
  9. NanoDrop. Typical yield ~1 µg/µL with A260/A280 ~2.0. Dilute to 20-50 ng/µL and use 1 µL per PCR. Qubit is preferable for quantification.
