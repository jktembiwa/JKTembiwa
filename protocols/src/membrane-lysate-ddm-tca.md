title: Lysate preparation for low-abundance membrane proteins (DDM extraction, TCA precipitation)
category: Protein biochemistry
tags: membrane proteins, lysis, DDM, TCA precipitation, M. smegmatis, western blot
summary: Bead-beat lysis with detergent extraction, then concentrate by TCA/acetone precipitation before SDS-PAGE.
source: Boutte Lab protocol (as received), adapted by K. Tembiwa.
version: Draft 0.1
---
Buffer W: 100 mM Tris pH 8.0, 150 mM NaCl, 1 mM EDTA.

1. Grow 10 mL *M. smegmatis* to late log (OD₆₀₀ 0.6–0.9). Put a conical of acetone at –20 °C.
2. Spin 5 min at 4 °C. Resuspend the pellet in 900 µL Buffer W + 1 mM PMSF (add 100 µL of 100 mM PMSF stock per mL; the source describes this as a 1:10 ratio). Transfer to a bead-beating tube pre-filled with beads. **No Tween** in the lysis buffer.
3. Bead-beat 2 min, ice 5 min, repeat for 4 rounds.
4. Add 120 µL 1% DDM (10 mg/mL in Buffer W) for 0.1% final. Bead-beat 1 min or vortex 1 min.
5. Spin 5 min at 16,000 rpm in the cold. Keep the supernatant; discard the pellet.
6. Measure A₂₈₀ (NanoDrop, blank: Buffer W + PMSF). Normalize all samples to the lowest concentration and bring to 1 mL.
7. Add 250 µL TCA (the source says "TCA", concentration not given; commonly 100% w/v stock). Incubate 10 min on ice. Spin max speed 5 min in the cold. Remove the supernatant without disturbing the pellet.
8. Wash with 200 µL cold acetone; spin 5 min in the cold; remove acetone. Repeat once.
9. Evaporate the acetone (open tubes or heat briefly).
10. Resuspend in 42 µL Buffer W + 8.3 µL 6× Laemmli. If the dye turns yellow (acidic), add 0.5 µL saturated NaOH and mix; repeat until blue and the pellet dissolves.
11. Heat 10 min at 95 °C and run on a gel. See [Western blot](western-blot-mycobacterial-membrane-proteins.html).
