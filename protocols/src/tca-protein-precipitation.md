category: Protein biochemistry

summary: Precipitate protein with 20% TCA, wash with cold acetone, dissolve in sample buffer and neutralize residual acid.

**Stock:** 100% (w/v) trichloroacetic acid: dissolve 500 g TCA (as shipped) in 350 mL dH₂O; store at room temperature (see Sambrook & Russell, *Molecular Cloning*, for details on TCA precipitation).

1. Add 1 volume TCA stock to 4 volumes of sample (e.g., 250 µL to 1.0 mL in a 1.5 mL tube; final ~20% TCA).
2. Incubate 10 min at 4 °C.
3. Spin 14,000 rpm, 5 min (microcentrifuge).
4. Remove the supernatant without disturbing the pellet (whitish, fluffy).
5. Wash with 200 µL cold acetone; spin 5 min.
6. Repeat the wash once more (two acetone washes total).
7. Dry the pellet by placing the open tube in a 95 °C heat block for 5–10 min to drive off the acetone.
8. For SDS-PAGE add 2× or 4× sample buffer (± β-mercaptoethanol) and boil 10 min at 95 °C.

**Residual acid:** if the sample buffer turns **brown/yellow instead of blue**, it is still acidic and will not dissolve or run well. Add 1 µL of saturated NaOH, pipette to mix, and repeat until it turns blue.

[The original says "saturated NaOH"; many labs use 1 M NaOH or Tris base instead. Confirm the concentration.]{.tbc}
