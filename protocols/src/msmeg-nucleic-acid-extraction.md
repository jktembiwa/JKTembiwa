Both protocols use hazardous reagents (chloroform, phenol, TRIzol). Work in a fume hood with appropriate protective equipment.

## Genomic DNA

1. Pellet 5–10 mL of culture.
2. Resuspend in 500 µL 2:1 chloroform:methanol and vortex for 30 s, 5 times.
3. Add 500 µL phenol and mix. Rock at room temperature for 30 min in a sealable tube.
4. Add 1 mL TE and rock for 1 hour at room temperature.
5. Spin at maximum speed for 3 min and move the upper aqueous phase to a new tube.
6. Add 1 volume isopropanol and 0.1 volume 3 M sodium acetate (pH 5.2).
7. Mix and spin at maximum speed for 10 min at 4 °C.
8. Wash the pellet with 200 µL 70% ethanol and pellet again for 5 min.
9. Air-dry the pellet and resuspend in water.

## RNA

1. Pellet 10 mL of culture.
2. Add 1 mL TRIzol and bead-beat 4 times for 40 s each.
3. Incubate at room temperature for 5 min.
4. Add 0.2 mL chloroform and shake for 15 s.
5. Centrifuge 15 min at 4 °C and transfer the upper aqueous phase to a new tube.
6. Add 500 µL ice-cold isopropanol and incubate at −20 °C for 15 min.
7. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 15 min at 4 °C and discard the supernatant.
8. Wash the pellet in 0.7 mL 70% ethanol (made in DEPC water).
9. Air-dry and resuspend in 100 µL DEPC water.
10. Add 350 µL Buffer RLT and 250 µL 100% ethanol and load onto an RNeasy column.
11. Follow the RNeasy spin instructions, stopping before the water elution.
12. Add 10 µL DNase I to 70 µL Buffer RDD, apply to the column and digest for 1 hour at room temperature.
13. Add 350 µL Buffer RW1, wait 5 min and centrifuge 15 s at 10,000 rpm.
14. Wash with Buffer RPE and elute following the kit instructions.
15. Digest the eluate again with TURBO DNase (buffer and 1 µL enzyme) at 37 °C for 30 min.
16. Add DNase inactivation reagent (1/10 of the volume), mix and incubate at room temperature for 5 min.
17. Centrifuge at 10,000 rpm for 5 min and transfer the RNA to a new tube for cDNA synthesis.
