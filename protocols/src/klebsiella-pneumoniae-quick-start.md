## About this page

This page is an orientation for working with *Klebsiella pneumoniae*. It has not yet been run in this lab, and most of it is general knowledge that has not been checked against a source, so highlighted text should be confirmed before use. For anything quantitative, follow a published protocol for your strain.

## Biosafety

[*K. pneumoniae* is normally handled at biosafety level 2. Check your institution's rules and the risk assessment for your strain, particularly for hypervirulent or carbapenem-resistant isolates.]{.tbc}

## Know your strain first

- [*K. pneumoniae* strains differ widely. Classical, hypervirulent and multidrug-resistant lineages behave differently, so identify the sequence type (ST), capsule (K) type and resistance genes before choosing antibiotics or a transformation method.]{.tbc}
- **Kleborate** is a genotyping tool for *K. pneumoniae* and its related species complex, described in Lam et al. (2021) as a genomic surveillance framework. [It takes an assembled genome and reports the species, ST, virulence factors, resistance genes and capsule type. Run it before you plan selection markers.]{.tbc} See [whole-genome sequencing analysis](whole-genome-sequencing-analysis.html) for assembling a genome.

## Culture

1. [Grow on LB or similar rich medium at 37 °C. Overnight cultures and colonies the next day are normal.]{.tbc}
2. [Check the strain's antibiotic susceptibility before picking a selection marker, because many clinical isolates are resistant to common antibiotics, and *K. pneumoniae* is intrinsically resistant to ampicillin.]{.tbc} Working concentrations for *E. coli* are in the [antibiotic table](antibiotic-plates.html), but [they may not apply to *Klebsiella*.]{.tbc}

## Capsule and hypermucoviscosity

- [The capsule is a major virulence factor and makes colonies glossy and mucoid.]{.tbc}
- [The **string test** is a quick check for hypermucoviscosity: touch a colony with a loop and lift. A string longer than about 5 mm is usually called positive. It is a phenotype, not a proof of hypervirulence.]{.tbc}
- [A thick capsule can lower transformation efficiency and make cells difficult to pellet. Expect to need to optimize.]{.tbc}

## Genetics

[Plasmid-based λ Red recombineering and conjugation from *E. coli* donors have been used in *Klebsiella*. Find a published protocol for your strain before starting, and use the [bi-parental conjugation page](bi-parental-conjugation.html) only as a model for the donor/counter-selection design, since that protocol was written for a different recipient.]{.tbc}
