## Materials

- 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes
- Magnetic stand for 1.5 mL tubes
- End-over-end rotator
- Pierce Anti-c-Myc Magnetic Beads (or equivalent)
- 1× TBS-T (25 mM Tris, 0.15 M NaCl, 0.05% Tween-20) and 5× TBS-T (125 mM Tris, 0.75 M NaCl, 0.25% Tween-20)

The amount of lysate and incubation time depend on how highly the tagged protein is expressed and need optimization. For co-IP, buffers must be chosen to preserve the interaction. Mix the bead stock thoroughly before use.

## Procedure

1. Place 25–100 µL beads in a tube. Add 175 µL 1× TBS-T and mix.
2. Collect beads on the magnet; discard supernatant.
3. Wash with 1 mL 1× TBS-T (invert or gently vortex 1 min). Collect and discard supernatant.
4. Add the sample (about 100 µg protein in 100 µL) and 400 µL 1× TBS-T. Incubate 30 min at room temperature with mixing.
5. Collect the beads. Save the unbound fraction for analysis.
6. Wash three times with 300 µL 5× TBS-T.
7. Wash once with 300 µL ultrapure water.

## Elution (choose one)

- **Gentle (peptide):** 100 µL c-Myc peptide at 0.5 mg/mL in TBS, 37 °C for 5–10 min on a rotator (lower temperature gives lower yield). Repeat once for better recovery. Use this if the protein must stay functional or is pH-sensitive.
- **Basic:** 100 µL 50 mM NaOH, 5 min at room temperature; neutralize with 50 µL neutralization buffer per 100 µL eluate.
- **Acidic:** 100 µL IgG elution buffer (pH 2.0) or 0.1 M glycine pH 2.0, 5–10 min at room temperature; neutralize with 15 µL neutralization buffer per 100 µL eluate.
- **SDS sample buffer:** 100 µL, 95–100 °C for 5–10 min. Non-reducing buffer reduces interference from co-eluting antibody fragments. For reducing conditions add 2.5 µL 2 M DTT per 100 µL.

Always include a negative control (beads with lysate from untagged cells) and keep the unbound fraction and washes for blotting.
