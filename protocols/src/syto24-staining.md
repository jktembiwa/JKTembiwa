**Reagent:** SYTO 24 green fluorescent nucleic acid stain (Thermo Fisher, catalog S7559). The stock is a liquid. Dilute 1:10 in DMSO and aliquot (2 µL and 10 µL) into PCR tubes. Store in a foil-covered box at –20 °C in a desiccator.

1. Stain with other dyes first (e.g. HADA) and complete their washes.
2. Add 1 µL diluted SYTO 24 to 100 µL culture. [The source says "Syto20" here; check that this is SYTO 24.]{.tbc}
3. Mount and image. No wash is needed.

The dye is very bright. Reduce exposure settings so images are not saturated (see [Image management](image-management-and-integrity.html)).
