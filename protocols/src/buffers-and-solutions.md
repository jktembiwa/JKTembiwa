## Stock dilutions

- **TAE:** dilute a 50X stock for running and pouring agarose gels.
- **PBS:** dilute a 10X stock.
- **Tris-glycine-SDS:** running buffer for tris-glycine gels; dilute from 10X.
- **Tris-glycine transfer buffer (SDS-PAGE):** dilute from 10X and add 10–20% methanol. 

## 10X PBS buffer (Phosphate-buffered saline) (1 L)

| Component | Amount | Final in 10X |
|---|---|---|
| NaCl (MW = 58.44 g/mol) | 80 g | 1.37 M |
| KCl (MW = 74.55 g/mol) | 2 g | 26.8 mM |
| Na₂HPO₄ (anhydrous, MW = 141.96 g/mol) | 14.4 g | 101.4 mM |
| KH₂PO₄ (MW = 136.09 g/mol) | 2.4 g | 17.6 mM |
| ddH₂O | Bring volume to 1 L | — |
| HCl / NaOH | Adjust to pH 7.4 | — |

## Preparation of 1 L 10X PBS buffer 

1. Measure approximately 700 mL of deionized water into a glass beaker or bottle.
2. Add 80 g of NaCl and stir until dissolved.
3. Add 2 g of KCl and stir until dissolved.
4. Add 14.4 g of anhydrous Na₂HPO₄.
5. Add 2.4 g of KH₂PO₄.
6. Stir until all components have dissolved completely.
7. Adjust the pH to 7.4 using HCl or NaOH.
8. Add deionized water to bring the final volume to 1 L.
9. Mix thoroughly.
10. Store at room temperature.

## Making a 1X working solution of 10X PBS

| Component | Amount | Final concentration |
|---|---|---|
| 10X PBS stock | 100 mL | 1X |
| ddH₂O | 900 mL | — |
| Total volume | 1 L | 1X PBS |

1. Measure 900 mL of distilled or deionized water into a suitable container.
2. Add 100 mL of 10X PBS stock solution.
3. Mix thoroughly.
4. The resulting 1X solution contains approximately 137 mM NaCl, 2.7 mM KCl, and 10 mM phosphate at pH 7.4.

## Preparation of 10X TBS buffer

1. Measure approximately 700 mL of deionized water into a glass beaker or bottle.
2. Add 24.23 g of Tris base.
3. Add 87.66 g of NaCl.
4. Stir until all components have dissolved completely.
5. Adjust the pH to 7.5 using HCl.
6. Add deionized water to bring the final volume to 1 L.
7. Mix thoroughly.
8. Store the stock solution at room temperature.

## Making a 1X working solution of 10X TBS

|---|---|---|

1. Measure 900 mL of distilled or deionized water into a suitable container.

## 1X TBS-T buffer (Tris-buffered saline with Tween-20) (1 L)

TBS-T is prepared by adding Tween-20 to 1X TBS. It is generally prepared as a working solution rather than a concentrated stock.

| Component | Amount | Final in 1X |
|---|---|---|
| 10X TBS stock | 100 mL | 1X |
| Tween-20 | 1 mL | 0.1% (v/v) |
| ddH₂O | Bring volume to 1 L | — |

## Preparation of 1X TBS-T buffer

1. Measure approximately 800–850 mL of deionized water into a glass beaker or bottle.
2. Add 100 mL of 10X TBS stock solution.
3. Add 1 mL of Tween-20.
4. Mix thoroughly until the Tween-20 is completely dispersed. Tween-20 is viscous, so pipette slowly.
5. Add deionized water to bring the final volume to 1 L.
6. Mix thoroughly until homogeneous.
7. Store at 4°C or room temperature according to laboratory practice.

## Alternative preparation using 1X TBS

| Component | Amount | Final in 1X |
|---|---|---|
| 1X TBS | 999 mL | 1X |
| Tween-20 | 1 mL | 0.1% (v/v) |
| Total volume | 1 L | 1X TBS-T |

1. Measure 999 mL of prepared 1X TBS.
2. Add 1 mL of Tween-20.
3. Mix thoroughly until homogeneous.
4. The resulting solution contains 20 mM Tris, 150 mM NaCl, and 0.1% Tween-20.

## 6X sample loading buffer - reducing (50 mL)

| Component | Amount | Final in 6X |
|---|---|---|
| 1M Tris-Cl pH 6.8 | 15 mL | 300 mM |
| SDS | 6 g | 12% (w/v) |
| Bromophenol blue | 0.3 g | 0.6% (w/v) |
| 100% Glycerol | 30 mL | 60% |
| DTT | 4.63 g (MW=154.24 g/mol) | 600 mM |
| ddH₂O | add up to 50 mL | 

## 6X Laemmli buffer (50 mL)

1. Combine the Tris-Cl, SDS, bromophenol blue and glycerol in the amounts indicated above.
2. When fully dissolved, cool on ice or in the fridge.
3. Add the DTT and stir quickly, keeping the solution on ice.
4. This laemmli buffer (sample loading buffer) is used for protein gel electrophoresis, usually at 1X final conc. in solution with your protein sample. 
5. For proteins, after adding sample buffer, heat the protein samples according to the requirements of your protein and electrophoresis system. A common starting condition is **95°C for 10 minutes**, although some proteins require alternative heating conditions.

## 50X Tris-Acetate-EDTA (TAE) buffer recipe (1 L)

| Component | Amount | Final in 50X |
|---|---|---|
| Tris base | 242 g (MW=121.14 g/mol) | 2 M |
| Glacial acetic acid | 57.1 mL | 1 M |
| 0.5 M EDTA pH 8.0 | 100 mL | 50 mM |
| ddH₂O | bring volume to 1L |

## 50X TAE buffer 

1. 1. Measure approximately 600 mL of deionized water into a glass graduated cylinder.
2. Add 242 g of Tris base and stir until dissolved.
3. Add 57.1 mL of glacial acetic acid. (Use a serological pipette inside a fume hood for safety and accuracy).
4. Add 100 mL of 0.5 M EDTA (pH 8.0) solution.
5. Add more deionized water to adjust the total final volume to 1 liter.
6. The pH of the stock solution does not need adjustment and should naturally sit around 8.5.
7. Store the stock solution at room temperature. 

## Making a 1X working stock of 50X TAE

1. Dilute 20mL of the 50X stock solution into 980mL of distilled or deionised water. 
2. The resulting 1X solution contains 40mM Tris, 20mM acetic acid, and 1mM EDTA.

## 10X Tris-Borate-EDTA (TBE) buffer recipe (1 L)

| Component | Amount | Final in 10X |
|---|---|---|
| Tris base | 108 g (MW=121.14 g/mol) | 890 mM |
| Boric acid | 55 g (MW=61.83 g/mol) | 890 mM |
| 0.5 M EDTA pH 8.0 | 40 mL | 20 mM |
| ddH₂O | bring volume to 1L |

## Prep of 10X TBE stock

1. Measure about 600 mL of DI water into a graduated cylinder.
2. Add 108 g of Tris base.
3. Add 55 g of boric acid. 
4. Stir (using stir bar and plate) until components have dissolved completely.
5. Add 40 mL of 0.5 M EDTA and stir thoroughly.
6. Add DI water to bring the final volume to 1L. Mix until homogenous and store at room temperature. 
7. Add 100 mL of 10X TBE stock into 900 mL of DI or ultrapure water to make 1X TBE buffer. 
8. The resulting 1X solution contains 89mM Tris, 89mM borate, and 2mM EDTA. 

**Important:** TBE is not interchangeable with TAE for every application. Borate can interfere with certain downstream enzymatic reactions, so TAE is often preferable when recovering DNA for subsequent molecular biology applications.

## 10X SDS-PAGE (Tris-Glycine-SDS) running buffer (1 L)

**Purpose:** Protein separation by molecular weight using denaturing SDS-PAGE. 

| Component | Amount | Final in 10X |
|---|---|---|
| Tris base | 30.3 g (MW=121.14 g/mol) | 250 mM |
| Glycine | 144.1 g (MW=75.07 g/mol) | 1.92 M |
| SDS | 10 g (MW=288.38 g/mol) | 1% (w/v) |
| ddH₂O | bring volume to 1L |

## Prep of 10X SDS-PAGE running buffer

1. Measure about 600 mL of DI water into glass grad. cylinder.
2. Add the above components in the indicated quantities.
3. Stir under dissolved before adding the 10 g of SDS. The stir with **very** gentle warming. 
4. Allow the solution to return to room temp, then add DI water to bring the final volume to 1L. Mix thoroughly and store at RT. 
5. Add 100 mL of 10X SDS-PAGE running buffer to 900 mL of DI water for working 1X SDS-PAGE running buffer. 
6. The resulting 1X solution contains 25mM Tris, 192mM glycine, and 0.1% SDS. 

**Important:** Do not adjust the pH of conventional Tris-glycine-SDS running buffer unless a specific electrophoresis system requires it.

## 10X Tris-Glycine western transfer buffer (1 L)

**Purpose:** Transfer of proteins from SDS-PAGE gels to nitrocellulose or PVDF membranes during Western blotting.

For this recipe, the concentrated stock contains Tris and glycine. Methanol is added during preparation of the working solution rather than stored in the concentrated stock! 

| Component | Amount | Final in 10X |
|---|---|---|
| Tris base | 30.3 g (MW=121.14 g/mol) | 250 mM |
| Glycine | 144.1 g (MW=75.07 g/mol) | 1.92 M |
| ddH₂O | bring volume to 1L |

## Prep of 10X Western transfer buffer

1. Measure about 600 mL of DI water into glass grad. cylinder.
2. Add the above components in the indicated quantities.
3. Stir under dissolved before adding the 10 g of SDS. The stir with **very** gentle warming. 
4. Allow the solution to return to room temp, then add DI water to bring the final volume to 1L. Mix thoroughly and store at RT. 

## Making 1X Western transfer buffer 

| Component | Amount | Final in 10X |
|---|---|---|
| 10X WTB | 100 mL | 1X |
| Methanol | 200 mL | 20% (v/v) |
| ddH₂O | bring volume to 1L |

1. Measure 700 mL of DI water into a suitable container. 
2. Add 100 mL of 10X WTB. 
3. Add 200 mL of methanol. Mix thoroughly. 
4. The resulting 1X solution contains 25mM Tris, 192mM glycine, and 20% methanol. 
5. SDS may be added when additional assistance with protein transfer is needed. **Do not add it normally.** If you do, add 0.5-1 g of SDS to the working (1X) buffer and mix until dissolved. 

**Important considerations:**

1. Methanol can improve protein binding to membranes but can reduce transfer efficiency for some high-molecular-weight proteins.
2. SDS can improve the transfer of certain proteins but may reduce protein binding to the membrane.
3. Methanol should be handled in a chemical fume hood.

## 20X SSC buffer (Saline-Sodium Citrate) (1 L)

**Purpose:** Southern and Northern blotting, nucleic acid transfer, membrane washing, and hybridization. 

| Component | Amount | Final in 20X |
|---|---|---|
| NaCl (Sodium chloride) | 175.3 g (MW=58.44 g/mol) | 3 M |
| Trisodium citrate dihydrate | 88.2 g (MW=294.1 g/mol) | 300 mM |
| ddH₂O | bring volume to 1L | 

## Prep of 20X SSC stock 

1. Measure about 600 mL of DI water into glass grad. cylinder.
2. Add the above components in the indicated quantities and stir until completely dissolved.
3. Adjust the pH to 7.0 using HCl. 
4. Add DI water to bring the final volume to 1L and mix thoroughly. Store at room temperature. 
4. For 1X, add 50 mL of 20X SSC stock to 950 mL of DI water. 
5. The resulting 1X solution contains 150mM NaCl and 15mM sodium citrate. 

## Electroporation wash solutions (10% glycerol; 10% glycerol + 500 mM sucrose)

Used to wash and resuspend electrocompetent *S. aureus* (Monk and Stinear 2021; see the [*S. aureus* quick-start](staphylococcus-aureus-quick-start.html)).

1. **10% glycerol:** mix 10 mL glycerol with 90 mL dH₂O.
2. **10% glycerol + 500 mM sucrose:** dissolve 17.1 g sucrose in 10% glycerol and bring to 100 mL.
3. [Sterilize by filtration (0.22 µm) and store at 4 °C.]{.tbc}
4. Use ice-cold.

## X-gal stock (blue/white screening)

1. [Dissolve X-gal at 20 mg/mL in dimethylformamide (DMF).]{.tbc}
2. [Store at −20 °C protected from light.]{.tbc}
3. Add to cooled agar to the working concentration. The pIMAY-Z protocol uses 100 µg/mL (Monk and Stinear 2021).
