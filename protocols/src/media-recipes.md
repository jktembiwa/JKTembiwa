## LB

See [Pouring LB plates](antibiotic-plates.html) for LB broth, agar and antibiotic concentrations.

## 7H9 (1 L)

**Filter sterilize. Do not autoclave.**

- 4.7 g 7H9 powder
- 2 mL glycerol (2.52 g)
- 2.5 mL 20% Tween 80
- 100 mL 10X ADC (or OADC)
- 900 mL dH₂O

Use ADC for *M. smegmatis* and OADC for *M. tuberculosis*.

## 10X ADC (1 L)

Supplies carbon (dextrose), toxin absorption (BSA) and radical scavenging (catalase). **Filter sterilize. Do not autoclave.**

- 50 g BSA fraction V ([Sigma brand]{.tbc})
- 20 g dextrose (D-glucose)
- 8.5 g NaCl
- 0.03 g catalase
- dH₂O to 1 L

## Sauton's (1 L)

A minimal medium, without ADC, for *M. smegmatis* and BCG. Cells grow more slowly than in 7H9, anhydrotetracycline induction may be weaker, and some strains (for example some conditional-depletion strains) may not grow in it.

- 0.5 g KH₂PO₄
- 0.5 g MgSO₄·7H₂O
- 2 g citric acid
- 0.05 g ferric ammonium citrate
- 60 mL glycerol
- 4 g asparagine
- dH₂O to 1 L

Adjust pH to 7.4 with 1 M NaOH. **Filter sterilize** (autoclaving destroys asparagine). Add 2.5 mL 20% Tween 80 to prevent clumping.

## Hartmans–de Bont (HdB) minimal medium (1 L)

A defined medium for *M. smegmatis* with growth similar to 7H9, useful for starvation experiments. Follow the order below to avoid a precipitate.

1. Add 10 mL of 100X M-stock to dH₂O.
2. Add the nitrogen source, 2 g (NH₄)₂SO₄ (15 mM), or omit for nitrogen starvation.
3. Add the carbon source, 2 mL glycerol (27.4 mM), or omit for carbon starvation.
4. Bring to 990 mL with dH₂O and autoclave.
5. Add 10 mL sterile 100X P-stock (or 100X P-starve stock for phosphate starvation).
6. For carbon starvation, add Tween 80 or tyloxapol to 0.05% (2.5 mL of a 20% sterile stock). Filter sterilize.

The medium must be autoclaved and then filter sterilized, or a precipitate forms over time.

**100X M-stock (100 mL):** EDTA 100 mg; MgCl₂·6H₂O 1000 mg; CaCl₂·2H₂O 10 mg; Na₂MoO₄·2H₂O 2 mg; CoCl₂·6H₂O 4 mg; MnCl₂·2H₂O 10 mg; ZnSO₄·7H₂O 20 mg; FeSO₄·7H₂O 50 mg; CuSO₄·5H₂O 2 mg.

**100X P-stock (100 mL):** K₂HPO₄ 15.5 g; NaH₂PO₄ 8.5 g. Autoclave.

**100X P-starve stock (100 mL):** K₂HPO₄ 0.155 g; NaH₂PO₄ 0.085 g; MOPS 10.46 g. Adjust to pH 7.0 and autoclave.
