Fast, but yield is lower and DNA more sheared than in longer protocols. Enough for 50–100 PCRs and some downstream uses (e.g. transposon mapping of isolated clones). See also [Nucleic acid extraction from M. smegmatis](msmeg-nucleic-acid-extraction.html).

Handle phenol and chloroform in a fume hood and dispose of them as hazardous waste.

1. Pellet 10 mL of overnight culture. Resuspend in 400 µL 10 mM Tris pH 9 and transfer to a bead-beating tube with glass beads.
2. Add 800 µL phenol:chloroform:isoamyl alcohol (25:24:1, pH 8.0). Bead-beat 2 × 45 s.
3. [Step 3 is missing in the source. Presumably a pause or spin before step 4.]{.tbc}
4. Spin 14,000 × g, 5 min. Transfer the upper aqueous layer (~150–300 µL; should be colorless) to a new tube.
5. Add 1 µL 1 mg/mL RNase A; 37 °C for 30 min. (RNA co-precipitates and interferes with A260/280 quantification; skip if this does not matter.)
6. [Steps 6–7 are missing in the source.]{.tbc}
7. Add 0.1 volume 3 M sodium acetate (pH ~7), mix.
8. Add 1 mL ice-cold ethanol, mix, and keep at –20 °C until you spin. Pellet 5–10 min at 14,000 × g in the cold.
9. Remove ethanol. Wash the pellet with 1 mL ice-cold 70% ethanol, spin 5 min at room temperature, aspirate. Wash 4–5 more times with 70% ethanol (no spin).
10. Spin 30 s to remove residual ethanol; dry 30 s to a few minutes. Resuspend in 50 µL water.
11. NanoDrop. Typical yield ~1 µg/µL with A260/A280 ~2.0 [(units as written in source; check)]{.tbc}. Dilute to 20 ng/µL and use 1 µL per PCR. Qubit is preferable for quantification.
