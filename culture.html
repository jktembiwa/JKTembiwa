Lab wiki

Good science, good to each other.

Culture is made in everyday decisions as much as in big statements.

Curiosity without ego

Ask questions early, make room for uncertainty, and treat unexpected results as useful information. No one is expected to know everything; everyone is expected to stay curious and willing to learn.

Rigor with generosity

Be careful with evidence and kind with people. Share methods and reasoning, give constructive feedback, acknowledge contributions, and distinguish a disappointing result from a disappointing person.

Respect and inclusion

Respect colleagues’ time, identities, boundaries, and different working styles. Make expectations explicit rather than assuming everyone has the same background knowledge or access to informal networks.

Psychological safety and accountability

People should be able to raise concerns, admit mistakes, and ask for help without humiliation. When something goes wrong, focus on safety and facts, then learning and prevention. Harmful conduct still needs to be addressed clearly and fairly.

Shared ownership

Lab culture is not the PI’s personality translated into rules. Invite feedback, revisit agreements, and make it possible for every team member to contribute to a healthier working environment.
