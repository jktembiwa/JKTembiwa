Resources for doing science—and doing it together.

A living library of useful readings, tools, guides, and career resources. The goal is to make good advice easier to find, evaluate, and put into practice.

How we’ll curate this page

  • Separate resources we have personally read or tested from suggestions that still need review.
  • For each resource, note who it may help, what question it addresses, and any limitations.
  • Prefer practical, accessible resources, and include different perspectives rather than relying on a single voice.
  • Revisit links periodically and replace material that becomes outdated.
  • Use institution-approved systems for sensitive records; this public page is not a place for confidential or unpublished lab material.
