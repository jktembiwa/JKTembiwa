A curated collection of useful readings, tools, guides, and career resources for rigorous research and a supportive scientific community.

Choosing useful resources

Useful resources make research practices clearer, support thoughtful mentoring, and help scientists make informed decisions. Consider each resource in context, including its evidence base, accessibility, intended audience, and limitations.
