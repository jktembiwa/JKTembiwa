News & updates.

Occasional notes about publications, research, conferences, teaching, and things I’m learning along the way.

2026

  1. Career

    Joined UiT The Arctic University of Norway as a postdoctoral researcher, working with Dr. Christian Lentz.

  2. Milestone

    Completed my PhD in Quantitative Biology at the University of Texas at Arlington.

  3. Publication

    First-author paper published in Journal of Bacteriology: PgfA regulates polar peptidoglycan synthesis as well as mycolate synthesis in Mycobacterium smegmatis. Read the paper ↗

  4. Talk

    Gave an oral presentation at ASM Microbe 2026 on PgfA and the coordination of the mycobacterial cell envelope.

  5. Award

    Received the Outstanding Graduate Research Achievement Award from UT Arlington.

  6. Award

    Awarded a Dissertation Fellowship from the UT Arlington Graduate School.

  7. Service

    Served as Vice President of the Biology Graduate Student Association (2025–2026).

2025

  1. Publication

    Co-authored a paper in Frontiers in Microbiology on how CwlM~P stimulates mycobacterial MurA through a charged interface distal from the phosphorylation site. I contributed to experimental design, molecular biology, protein purification, in vitro enzyme assays and characterization of interface mutants.

  2. Talk

    Gave an oral presentation at the SACNAS National Diversity in STEM Conference on PgfA as a potential regulator of coordination between mycolic acid and peptidoglycan synthesis.

  3. Award

    Received the Carl Storm Underrepresented Minority Fellowship from the Gordon Research Conferences.

  4. Service

    Served as President of the SACNAS chapter at UT Arlington (2024–2025).

2024

  2. Award

  3. Talk
