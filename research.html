Questions first. Cells in context.

My background is in bacterial cell-envelope biology, with developing interests in the diversity of microbial states and behaviors.

Research background

Building and regulating the bacterial cell envelope

My doctoral work examined how peptidoglycan synthesis and cell-wall assembly are regulated in Mycobacterium smegmatis. This included studies of MurJ, a lipid II flippase, and PgfA, a factor involved in coordinating polar peptidoglycan and mycolate synthesis.

Developing direction

Single-cell heterogeneity

A developing research direction is to investigate how individual bacterial cells differ in physiological and enzymatic states, what molecular processes drive those differences, and whether they relate to responses to environmental stress or antibiotics.
