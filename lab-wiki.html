A lab should help people do their best work.

A growing guide to the science we want to do, the community we want to build, and the practices that help people thrive while doing rigorous, exciting research.

Our north star

Be adventurous in the questions we ask and rigorous in the answers we claim.

We want to pursue discoveries that open new directions while staying honest about what the evidence supports. We want people to become thoughtful, independent experts who can challenge ideas, collaborate generously, and shape their own futures. Scientific excellence matters; so do dignity, fairness, inclusion, and the people doing the work.

Principles, practices, and policies

As this wiki develops, we’ll distinguish between:

  • Principles: what we believe and value.
  • Practices: how we try to put those values into action.
  • Policies: specific expectations, procedures, and consequences that need careful definition.
  • Resources: tools and references that help us make decisions and improve.

Some ideas are already clear; others need discussion. We will not treat an unfinished idea as settled policy, and any formal lab procedures must be aligned with institutional requirements and developed with the people they affect.

Public-site note: This repository is public. This wiki is a set of evolving, public-facing drafts—not an authenticated internal handbook or a substitute for institutional policy. Do not publish private lab records, sensitive personnel information, unpublished findings, credentials, or restricted data here.
