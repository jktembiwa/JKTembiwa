Lab wiki

A lab should help people do their best work.

A starter collection of ideas for how a future research group can work together, look after one another, and make good science easier to do.

Culture & values

Curiosity, generosity, rigor, inclusion, and respect for different working styles.

Group handbook & operations

Practical working agreements for communication, meetings, authorship, safety, and joining or leaving the group.

Knowledge-sharing

Document methods clearly, record decisions, explain the reasoning behind protocols, and make useful resources findable.

Keep improving

Working agreements should be revisited with the people who use them. The goal is clarity and fairness, not rules for their own sake.

These are public starter drafts, not adopted institutional policies. This site is public; never put confidential lab records, unpublished results, sensitive incident reports, or restricted data here.
